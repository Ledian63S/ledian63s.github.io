Dëgjo

iOS App · 2026

An iOS audio learning app built for visually impaired users — and specifically, by a son for his father. Dëgjo delivers 285 English lessons controlled entirely through gestures, with no screen required. A six-gesture system covers everything: play/pause, restart, skip, lesson change, and voice-based selection. Albanian voice announcements confirm every action, and the app always resumes exactly where the user left off.
