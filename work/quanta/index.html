Quanta

Flutter App · 2026

A cross-platform position size calculator built for futures traders on Quantower. Enter your account balance, risk per trade, and stop loss — Quanta computes the exact contract count instantly. Includes a full risk ladder at your fixed stop, a manual contract override, and persistent settings across sessions. Gold-on-black terminal aesthetic with a scanline overlay.
