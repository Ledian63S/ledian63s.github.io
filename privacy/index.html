What is collected

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymised data about how visitors interact with pages — including pages visited, time spent, scroll depth, and outbound link clicks. No personally identifiable information is collected. IP addresses are anonymised by Google before storage.

Legal basis

Analytics are only activated after you explicitly accept cookies via the banner. Your consent can be withdrawn at any time by clicking "Customize" in the cookie banner (shown on your next visit after clearing your preference) or by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

Retention

Google Analytics retains event data for 14 months by default. Your cookie preference is stored locally in your browser until you clear it.
