Quanta
Flutter App · 2026

A cross-platform futures position size calculator built for Quantower traders. Input your account balance, risk per trade, and stop loss — Quanta instantly computes the exact number of contracts. Includes a full risk ladder at your fixed stop loss, a contract override for manual adjustments, and persistent settings across sessions. Gold-on-black terminal aesthetic with a scanline overlay.
